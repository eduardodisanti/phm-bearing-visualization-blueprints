Bearing Blueprints

Interactive visualization of bearing degradation from detection to prognosis

Overview

Bearing Blueprints is an interactive visual analytics project developed for the DTSA 5304 Fundamentals of Data Visualization at the University of Colorado Boulder.

The project uses the NASA IMS Bearing Dataset to investigate how visualization can help engineers detect degradation, understand anomalous behaviour and support predictive maintenance decisions.

Research Questions

  • Can users distinguish normal behaviour from degradation?
  • Can they identify when degradation begins?
  • Can visual evidence explain why a bearing is anomalous?
  • Can degradation trends support exploratory prognosis?
